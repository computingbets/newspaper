Poetry: Look @ Me Generation

by J Cohen

The 1970’s ushered in the ‘me’ generation

A term coined by journalist Tom Wolfe

As we flicker about our phones, time has elapsed But yielded the same existential frustrations

Yet the mode of the culture has shifted while it has passed

I believe we live in the ‘look at me generation’

Our products and outlets and outlooks Combat

The simple grind to get the spoils of any battle

Because our focus and obsession with getting attention has surpassed

The owning of that car or the winning like a star

There’s a need to know the frequency of a glow And to tell Facebook where you are

When you wish upon a star you need more than 30 likes by far

Branding and standing in a selfie with your bestie And the tattoo without a scar

These are all a few of our favorite things Provided it’s in the right filter you needed thus far

And it’s just what it is. I’m no critic. I’m just a guy

I don’t use social media but not to be the guy Who doesn’t use social media and then tell you why It’s all better than no communication Though it can be fast, frequent, flashy And you feel you must comply

Because there are billions posting a Gif or for any myriad of reasons A mascot like the monopoly guy

Just to say “hey world, look into my eye”.
